In short

  • The apps never send your recordings or text to any server.
  • No account required, and no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Cloud features (Pro) are not available yet — once they launch, they only work when you turn them on.

Data in the app

WhisperInc for Mac and iPhone turns speech into text with models that run on your device. Recordings, transcripts, results and your edits, modes, replacements, history and insights are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to any server, and we have no access to them. No account is required.

You decide how long recordings are kept and whether audio is kept at all. You can delete single recordings or all stored audio at any time. Text you copy goes to the system clipboard; text you share goes to the app you choose in the share sheet.

The apps connect to the internet only for downloads and updates, never to process your speech:

  • Model downloads. When you choose a model that is not built into the system, its files are downloaded from Hugging Face, which handles the request under its own privacy policy.
  • Updates. WhisperInc for Mac checks the update feed on this website when update checking is turned on. WhisperInc for iPhone is updated through the App Store.

The apps contain no ads, no analytics and no tracking. If you have chosen in your Apple device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports of the App Store version on to us.

Account and sync (Pro)

There is no account and no sync today. When WhisperInc Pro launches, signing in and sync will be optional. Sync will transfer your modes, replacements and — only if you enable it — the text of your history. Audio recordings will always stay on your device. We will update this policy with the details before these features become available.

Cloud models (Pro)

Today every model runs on your device. When Pro launches, you will be able to turn on a cloud model for a chosen mode; only recordings from that mode will then be sent for processing. Before that happens, this policy will say who processes them and how long they are kept. We will never use your recordings to train models.

Prompt suggestions (Pro)

Prompt suggestions are not available yet. When Pro launches, WhisperInc will be able to suggest better instructions for a mode by comparing the AI result with your edit. The feature will be optional, and you will be able to turn it off in Settings.

This website

This is a static website. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party page resources, and it has no account or form for submitting personal information. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes technical data needed to deliver pages, such as your IP address, under its own privacy statement. If you follow an external link or email us, that destination handles your visit or message under its own practices.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and export your personal data, to object to its processing and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). Because there is no account, we hold no personal data about you unless you email us. Data in the app stays on your device, and you can delete it there at any time.
