How it works

Speak freely. Get finished text.

Behind the scenes, WhisperInc does a few things at once, so your text arrives faster and needs fewer fixes.

  1. Talk the way you talk

    No need to choose words carefully. A rewriting mode cleans up ums, stumbles and repeats.

  2. No waiting around

    Silence is trimmed from the recording, so your text is ready sooner.

  3. Accurate transcription

    Whisper models understand English and dozens of other languages.

  4. Text in your style

    If you like, AI rewrites it as an email, a message or a note.

  5. Ready to paste

    The text goes straight to your clipboard.

Modes

One tone for your boss, another for your friends.

Modes are presets for different situations. Emails sound professional, messages stay casual, transcripts stay word for word. Create your own modes with your own instructions.

Example mode
You say

“Notes for Thursday: the whisper ink launch moves to May, Ola owns the checklist, and we still need the new price list from finance.”

You get

Notes for Thursday: the WhisperInc launch moves to May, Ola owns the checklist, and we still need the new price list from finance.

Word for word — only your replacement “whisper ink” → “WhisperInc” is applied.

You say

“Uh tell Kuba I’m running like ten minutes late, the tram’s stuck again, so maybe just order for me, the usual.”

You get

Running about 10 minutes late, the tram’s stuck again. Could you order for me? The usual. Thanks!

You say

“Okay so um tell Marta thanks for the Q3 draft, it’s, it reads well, but uh could we move the hiring bit after the budget and also add last quarter’s numbers, and maybe we jump on a call tomorrow.”

You get

Hi Marta, Thanks for the Q3 draft — it reads well. Could we move the hiring section after the budget and add last quarter’s numbers for comparison? Happy to jump on a call tomorrow. Best, Jakub

You say

“So um for the team, quick update, the demo went fine, the client really liked the history view, uh they want a Polish version by Friday, and yeah let’s sync on Monday.”

You get

Hi team, quick update: the demo went well and the client really liked the history view. They’d like a Polish version by Friday. Let’s sync on Monday.

You say

“Okay so tomorrow I need to call the dentist, send the invoice to Anna and uh buy a birthday gift for Tomek.”

You get

• Call the dentist • Send the invoice to Anna • Buy a birthday gift for Tomek

Your own “To-do list” mode with one instruction: turn what I say into a short checklist.

FAQ

Common questions

Which languages does WhisperInc understand?

It depends on the speech model you choose. Whisper models understand 99 languages, including English, Polish, German, Spanish and French; Parakeet Ultra covers 25 European languages. The app itself is in English and Polish.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once a model is downloaded, all speech-to-text happens on your Mac or iPhone. You only need a connection to download models and app updates.

Do I need an account?

No. WhisperInc works without signing in and has no accounts today. Accounts will only come with the optional Pro plan later.

Does my audio go to the cloud?

No. Audio, transcripts and history stay on your device — WhisperInc has no server. Cloud models are planned as an optional part of Pro, later.

Which devices does WhisperInc support?

Macs with Apple Silicon running macOS 26 or later, and iPhones running iOS 26 or later.
