WhisperInc at a glance

A concise, quotable fact sheet about WhisperInc, its platforms, privacy and on-device models.

WhisperInc facts

  • WhatWhisperInc turns speech into ready-to-use text with local speech and text models.
  • PlatformsWhisperInc is a native app for macOS and iOS.
  • Minimum OSmacOS 26 or later on Apple Silicon; iOS 26 or later.
  • PriceThe v1 apps and local models are free; no account is required.
  • LanguagesThe app interface is in English and Polish. Speech and text languages depend on the selected model.
  • PolishWhisper and Parakeet Ultra support Polish transcription; Qwen 3.5 and Gemma 4 provide Polish rewriting options. Apple Speech and Apple Intelligence do not support Polish.
  • PrivacyAudio, transcripts and history stay on the user's device in v1. The app has no account or backend.
  • Offline useThe app works offline after downloadable models have been installed. Downloading models and app updates requires an internet connection.
  • ModelsLocal speech models include Whisper, Parakeet and Apple Speech. Local text models include Qwen 3.5, Gemma 4 and Apple Intelligence where supported.
  • WebsiteThe product website uses no analytics, cookies or third-party requests.
