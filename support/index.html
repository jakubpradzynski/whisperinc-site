Support

Troubleshooting help for WhisperInc on Mac and iPhone.

Contact

Email: WI_PLACEHOLDER_SUPPORT_EMAIL. Please include your device, operating-system version, app version, what you were doing, and the exact error text. Do not email recordings or other private text unless you have chosen to share it.

Troubleshooting

Recording does not start

  • On Mac, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and allow WhisperInc to use the microphone.
  • On iPhone, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and allow WhisperInc.
  • Close and reopen WhisperInc, then try again. Check that the intended microphone is connected and available.

Paste does not work on Mac

Automatic paste requires Accessibility permission. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and allow WhisperInc. You can still copy a result and paste it yourself if you do not want to grant this permission.

A model download fails

Check your internet connection and available device storage, then retry the download from Models. Model files are downloaded from Hugging Face. If it continues to fail, send Support the error text and the model name; do not send model files.

Start fresh or remove local data

WhisperInc does not provide an in-app reset-all command. You can manage recordings and audio in History and General, and remove installed models from Models. If you need help with a broader reset, contact Support before removing the app so we can help you understand what local data may be affected.
