FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about WhisperInc, its platforms, model choices, privacy and offline use.

Find standalone answers about WhisperInc, app availability, model choices, privacy and offline use.

What is WhisperInc?

+

WhisperInc is a native macOS and iOS app that turns speech into text using on-device models. The v1 apps are in development.

Does WhisperInc work offline?

+

WhisperInc works offline after the models you choose have been downloaded. An internet connection is needed to download models and receive app updates.

How much does WhisperInc cost?

+

WhisperInc v1 and its local models are free. There is no account or subscription required for local features.

Which languages does WhisperInc support?

+

The app interface is available in English and Polish. Speech recognition and rewriting languages depend on the selected model; Whisper supports 99 languages, while Parakeet Ultra supports 25 European languages.

Can I transcribe Polish speech?

+

Yes. Whisper models support Polish, and Parakeet Ultra includes Polish among its 25 European languages. Apple Speech does not support Polish.

Can WhisperInc rewrite text in Polish?

+

Yes. Qwen 3.5 and Gemma 4 are local text-model options for Polish rewriting. Apple Intelligence does not support Polish.

Which devices can run WhisperInc?

+

WhisperInc v1 targets Macs with Apple Silicon running macOS 26 or later and iPhones running iOS 26 or later. Individual models have additional memory and language requirements.

Does WhisperInc support Intel Macs?

+

No. WhisperInc for Mac requires Apple Silicon and does not support Intel Macs.

How much storage do the models need?

+

Model downloads range from 77 MB for Whisper Tiny to several gigabytes for larger models. Apple Speech and Apple Intelligence are system models and have no model download; see the model catalog for sizes.

Does WhisperInc send my recordings to a server?

+

No. In v1, audio, transcripts and history stay on your device; WhisperInc has no account or backend. Model files are downloaded from Hugging Face when you choose to install them.

Where are my recordings and transcripts stored?

+

WhisperInc stores recordings and text in the app's local data on your device. The app lets you choose how long to keep recordings and whether to keep audio files.

Can I delete my recordings and data?

+

Yes. You can delete recordings from History and configure retention and audio-file storage in General settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data according to the operating system's app-removal behavior.

How do I paste a result into another Mac app?

+

WhisperInc copies a result to the clipboard when the selected mode is set to copy. In the directly distributed Mac build, the Paste last result shortcut can send Command-V when Accessibility permission is granted; without permission, paste from the clipboard yourself.

Does WhisperInc record in the background on iPhone?

+

No. In v1, iPhone recording opens the WhisperInc app and starts there; background recording is not included.

Can I use the iPhone Action Button, Control Center or widgets?

+

WhisperInc plans quick-start entry points through App Shortcuts, Control Center and widgets on supported iPhones. These entry points open the app to start recording; they do not provide background recording in v1.

Are cloud models, sync or accounts available?

+

No. Cloud models, accounts, cross-device sync and prompt suggestions are planned for later and are not available in v1.
