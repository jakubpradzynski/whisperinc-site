Features

Features

Record speech, shape it into ready-to-use text and keep every result private on your devices.

WhisperInc turns a spoken thought into text you can use. Record in a native app, choose a mode for the job, and keep the result in your private history.

Speak once, get text ready for the task

The built-in Transcription mode keeps the recognized words. Custom modes can optionally rewrite a transcript as a short message, a professional email, or a voice-note script. Each mode has its own instructions and can use the speech and text models available on your device.

Choose your models

Pick a speech-to-text model and, when rewriting is enabled, a text-to-text model. The catalog includes open-weight models that run locally and Apple system models where supported. The app shows each model’s size, speed, accuracy, supported languages, and device requirements.

Make transcripts sound like you

Optional silence trimming removes pauses before processing. Personal replacements apply rules such as whisper ink → WhisperInc or removing a filler phrase. A mode can copy its result to the clipboard when processing finishes.

Find, replay and edit past recordings

History keeps transcripts and generated results as separate versions. You can search past recordings, replay their audio when it was retained, and save an edit as another version without overwriting the original text. Insights summarize recording time, words, modes, replacements and edits.

A native workflow on each device

On macOS, WhisperInc lives in the menu bar, supports global keyboard shortcuts, and shows a floating recording and processing indicator. In the direct Mac build, the paste-last-result shortcut can paste into another app when Accessibility permission is granted; otherwise, the text is copied for manual pasting.

On iPhone, recording starts from a single-button home screen. App Shortcuts, Control Center and widgets provide quick entry points. The iOS app opens for recording; it does not record in the background in v1.

Private by design

In v1, audio, transcripts and history stay on your device. There is no account or backend, and the app works offline after any chosen models are downloaded. You control how long recordings are kept and whether audio files are retained. The optional future cloud, sync and prompt-suggestion features are not part of v1.

Later, not in v1

Accounts, cross-device sync, cloud models and AI prompt suggestions based on edits are planned for a later version. They are not active services in v1.
