One-liner

WhisperInc is a voice-to-text app for Mac, with the iPhone app coming soon.

Longer description

WhisperInc lets you talk instead of type. Start a recording with a keyboard shortcut on Mac, then let on-device models turn your speech into text and prepare it as an email, message or note. Your finished text is ready to copy or share. No account is required. Model downloads need an internet connection; speech processing runs on your device. The iPhone app is coming soon. WhisperInc Pro, cloud models, sync and prompt suggestions are planned for later.
